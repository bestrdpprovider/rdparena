Got questions?

Answers Before You Click Buy

The most common questions our team gets from new customers.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major payment methods so you can pay the way you prefer:
PayPal Credit Card Debit Card Bitcoin (BTC) Ethereum (ETH) Tether (USDT)
How quickly is my RDP set up after payment?
Most plans are provisioned within a few hours of confirmed payment, with a maximum setup window of 4 hours. In the rare case we need to spin up a fresh node for stock reasons, you will hear from us promptly with a clear timeline.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a 24-hour money-back guarantee on new orders. If something is not working for you within the first 24 hours, open a ticket and our team will process your refund.
Do I get full admin access to install my own software?
Full administrator access is not provided directly. However, if you need any software installed (trading platforms, browsers, accounting suites, productivity tools and so on), simply raise a support ticket and our team will install it on your server for you, free of charge.
Which operating system do RDPs run on?
All our RDPs come with licensed Windows Server builds. If you need a specific Windows version, just mention it in your order notes or open a support ticket and we will accommodate where possible.
Can I upgrade or change my plan later?
Yes, plan upgrades and changes are handled on a pro-rated basis at the discretion of our staff. Open a support ticket explaining what you need, and our team will calculate the pro-rated adjustment and move you to the new plan.
Is 24/7 support really 24/7?
Yes. Our ticket system is monitored around the clock by real humans. Most replies come back within a short window, regardless of your time zone.
Where are your servers physically located?
We have 8 global locations across the United States (5 cities), London in the United Kingdom, and Netherlands. Pick the location closest to the service or markets you work with for lowest latency.
How secure is my data on an RDP Arena server?
Payments are processed over SSL-encrypted channels. RDP sessions themselves use encrypted protocols, and each customer gets isolated environments with their own credentials.
What is your acceptable use policy?
RDP Arena services are for legal business and personal productivity use only. We do not allow spam, abuse, scanning, or any activity that violates the laws of the server's jurisdiction or our Terms of Service. Misuse may result in suspension without refund.
